## 2024-06-01 - Tooltips for textual action buttons
**Learning:** While textual action buttons provide a basic label, screen readers and users benefit from a more descriptive `help` tooltip that explains the outcome of the action (e.g., "Save changes" instead of just "Save"), enhancing both accessibility and usability across the app.
**Action:** Add descriptive `help` kwarg tooltips to standard action buttons (Save, Cancel, Back, Send) to provide deeper context for interactions.
